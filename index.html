Privacy Policy for Timer Game

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Timer Game ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mobile game designed for entertainment and timed challenges.

Information We Collect

Timer Game does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The app stores certain game and reward-related information locally on the user's device so that game settings, counters, and reward availability can persist between app sessions.

Advertising

Timer Game uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect and use information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about ad interactions to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising services, subject to Google's policies and the user's device and privacy settings.

For more information about how Google handles information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy.

Rewarded Advertisements

Users may voluntarily watch rewarded advertisements to receive temporary ad-free access within Timer Game. The app stores information necessary to manage reward availability and the temporary ad-free period.

Local Storage

Timer Game uses local device storage to preserve certain app information, including game-related counters and rewarded-ad availability. This information remains on the user's device and is not used to create a user account.

Third-Party Services

Timer Game uses Google AdMob for advertising. Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Timer Game is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.
